Compositor Scroll Overlay

Powered by CSS functions (margin-top: calc(100vh) and animation-timeline: scroll()). No empty spacer divs in the DOM.

Compositor Scroll Sheet

How it works: The sheet uses margin-top: calc(100vh) to create the scroll runway. When you scroll or pull down at the top, the browser compositor translates the sheet downward. If released after moving ≥ 30%, it dismisses; if < 30%, it smoothly springs back.

1. Native Compositor Gestures

The entire scroll interaction runs directly on Chromium's compositor thread. There is no JavaScript drag physics or touch coordinate computation, ensuring smooth 120Hz performance.

2. Fluid Bidirectional Scrubbing

You can drag or scroll down into the transparent space, pause, push it back up, or scrub freely in either direction. The sheet tracks your input 1:1 without premature snapping.

3. Scrollable Article Body

When reading content, the header sticks cleanly to the top of the viewport. Scrolling down navigates through the article content naturally.
